## 📊 Task 2 — Data Visualization & Storytelling (Tableau)

### Objective
To analyze and communicate sales and profit insights using visual storytelling and dashboard design.

### Dataset Used
Superstore Sales Dataset (Sample - Tableau)

### Visualizations Created
1. Sales Trend Over Time (Line Chart)
2. Sales by Category (Bar Chart)
3. Profit by Region (Filled Map)
4. Sales by Segment (Bar Chart)
5. Top 10 Products by Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart)
6. Combined Dashboard
7. Story Presentation

### Tools Used
- Tableau Public (macOS)

### Deliverables
- `ElevateLabs_Superstore_Insights_and_Visualization_Report.pdf`
- Dashboard & Story screenshots (optional)
- Tableau workbook (.twbx) (optional)

### Key Findings (Short Summary)
- Technology category leads in revenue contribution.
- Western states generate highest profit; some central regions require intervention.
- Consumer segment dominates overall sales share.
- Top-performing products mainly belong to Technology & Office Supplies categories.
